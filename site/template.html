Bonsai Neuron Survey

Every neuron in a 1-bit language model, on one sheet. Your AI agent does the work: draw a neuron, paste one prompt into Claude Code or Codex, and it runs the test on your GPU and opens the pull request. Every label here was tested with sentences committed before the test ran.

NewSteering with globalMOO: labelled neurons as 24 knobs on the model's writing, solved as one multiobjective problem across nine pre-registered rounds→ StreamCircuits from the stream: five places where neurons mapped live on Twitch line up into something you can read like a program→

Testing the claim of Nick Cammarata's Neural Networks Are Already Clean (Goodfire, 2026) that most native neurons have one main job, against the dictionary-learning view of Anthropic's Towards Monosemanticity (Bricken et al., 2023).

A project by Matt Freeman · @moridinamael on X · LinkedIn

How far the map has gotBonsai-8B has 442,368 MLP neurons (36 layers × 12,288). A neuron's output can be positive or negative, and the two directions often do different jobs, so each direction, a pole, is labelled on its own: 884,736 poles in all. A label is assignment-grade when it passed its test on held-out text and explains at least half of the pole's strong activity.

Survey labels

Written by people and their AI agents, committed before the test ran, then tested and merged through a pull request.

Automatic labels

From deterministic passes over the corpus; no language model writes them. Each is a hypothesis tested on held-out text, kept apart from the survey's labels.

What you send back

Interpret

  • <id>.tests.json: your label, 8 sentences that should fire it, and 8 near misses. Committed first, on its own.
  • <id>.json: the results written by play run. Committed second.
  • Held when at least 5 of 8 fire at 0.3 of the pole's corpus maximum and at most 1 near miss does.

QC

  • New sentences for an existing label: confirm it as it stands, dispute it with a test built to break it, or refine it with a better label.
  • A confirm that fails counts as a dispute. When disputes outnumber confirmations, the pole is marked contested.

Contributions are append-only. A merge never edits anyone's work; each neuron's record is rebuilt from all of its contributions.

Before your first neuron

Start your agent

Paste this into Claude Code, Codex or any agent that can run a shell. It clones the repositories, installs everything, and keeps going until the readiness check passes.

You need an NVIDIA GPU with 8 GB of memory (or an Apple Silicon Mac), about 8 GB of disk, and a GitHub account. The agent asks you only for what it can't do itself: logging in to GitHub, your handle, the disk space, and an optional globalMOO key. The procedure lives in the repository's AGENTS.md, so this prompt stays current.
